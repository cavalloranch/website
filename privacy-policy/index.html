Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 12, 2026

Cavallo Ranch operates this website and may use third-party contractors to support service delivery. This Privacy Policy applies to visitors and describes our data collection, use, and disclosure practices. Cavallo Ranch acts as the data controller, with Service Providers functioning as data processors.

1. Personal Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Contact details (name, email, address, phone)
  • Feedback and correspondence
  • Marketing preferences and communication engagement details
  • Other voluntary information

Information From Third Parties

  • Social media platform data
  • Property records, demographic data, and behavioral information
  • Analytics services, marketing partners, and publicly available sources

CRM and Communication Data

  • Email content, headers, metadata, attachments
  • Text message content and metadata
  • Communication timestamps and delivery status
  • Interaction notes and contact history

Automatic Data Collection

  • Device information (OS, browser type, IP address, location)
  • Online activity (pages viewed, browsing history, email opens)
  • Cookies, local storage, and web beacons

2. How Personal Information Is Used

Cavallo Ranch uses personal information to:

  • Provide and operate Services
  • Manage customer relationships via CRM tools
  • Send follow-up and marketing communications
  • Comply with legal requirements
  • Prevent fraud and ensure safety
  • Create anonymous data for business purposes

3. Use of Artificial Intelligence

The company uses AI for:

  • Generating property descriptions and marketing content
  • Assisting with communications and responses
  • Analyzing data and providing recommendations

Your AI Rights: Users may request information about AI usage, ask for human review of significant decisions, opt out of certain AI features, and provide feedback.

4. How Personal Information Is Shared

Cavallo Ranch shares data with:

  • Internal team, partners, and affiliates
  • Service Providers (hosting, IT, CRM, analytics)
  • Professional advisors (lawyers, auditors, insurers)
  • Compliance and safety purposes
  • Business transfer situations

The company does not sell personal information and never discloses phone numbers or SMS consent to third parties for marketing.

5. Advertising and Tracking Technologies

Cavallo Ranch uses tracking pixels and shares information with advertising platforms (Meta, Google) to deliver targeted ads, measure campaign effectiveness, and create lookalike audiences. Users may encounter interest-based advertising on third-party websites.

Cavallo Ranch retains personal information as long as necessary for service delivery and legal compliance. Contact information is retained during the relationship and thereafter for follow-up communications. CRM data remains while the relationship is active. When information is no longer needed, it is securely deleted or anonymized.
