Everything to do, all on the grounds

The Ranch

Twenty acres of play

Cavallo Ranch is built for the way groups actually spend their time — together, outdoors, and never bored. Enjoy countless complimentary activities, with all equipment provided. You bring the company.

Swim, Splash and Lounge

A private lake, pool, and water toys

Paddleboard the man-made swim lake, cool off in the infinity pool, or lounge on the deck as the desert glows — the estate's natural gathering point, from first coffee to a nightcap under the stars.

Sports and Activities

Tennis, bocce, beach volleyball

Complimentary activities run all day — paddleboarding, tennis, bocce, and beach volleyball among them. Every bit of equipment is provided and ready to go.

Eat & Drink

Best Table in Town

Dine beneath the stars with al fresco dining, a fully equipped outdoor kitchen, and traditional parrilla made for unforgettable gatherings. Enjoy cocktails at the popular Polo Bar, or let our concierge arrange a private chef and dedicated butler service to create an extraordinary dining experience in the comfort of the ranch. From casual poolside lunches to elegant dinners under the desert sky, every meal becomes part of the Cavallo experience.

Hang Space

Gather & Unwind

Cavallo Ranch offers an array of inviting spaces designed for groups to connect, relax, and make memories together. Gather for cocktails at the Polo Bar, settle into the spacious Great Room, or enjoy a more casual evening in the comfortable family area off the chef's kitchen. From lively gatherings to quiet conversations, every space is designed to bring your crew together.

Concierge Services

Your wish is our command

Our proficient concierge team crafts a bespoke stay reflective of your unique preferences — from a personalized grocery service stocking the fridge with gourmet delights, to an adventurous outing amidst the vistas of La Quinta, a whimsical bouncy castle for younger guests, or a mechanical bull for a touch of playful elegance.

Equestrian Activities

A four-acre polo field

Make the most of four acres of impeccably manicured grass and create your own adventure. Take a private polo lesson, saddle up for an equestrian experience, set up a soccer match, or simply let the kids run free. With expansive open space and the spirit of the ranch all around you, Cavallo offers endless possibilities for play, sport, and unforgettable moments together.
