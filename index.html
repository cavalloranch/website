Cavallo Ranch is an exclusive luxury estate in California's Coachella Valley, where extraordinary vacations, unforgettable celebrations, and world-class events come to life. Location

Convenience and exclusivity

Nestled at the strategic crossroads of La Quinta, Indio, and Thermal, Cavallo Ranch offers both. Within its secure 20-acre confines, guests savor tranquility while being mere moments away from vibrant attractions. Reach Thermal Airport for a private charter in just five minutes, or Palm Springs International Airport in thirty-five.

From here you can easily explore Joshua Tree National Park, entertain the young ones at The Living Desert, or hit the greens at numerous local golf courses. Thrill-seekers have Thermal Racing Club nearby; shoppers can wander El Paseo; tennis enthusiasts might catch the BNP Paribas Open at Indian Wells. And during festival season, Cavallo Ranch is a stone's throw from the Coachella Valley Music and Arts Festival.
